What this service is

This service lets registered users manage a Yu-Gi-Oh! card collection and decks, and optionally publish a public trade list. It is not affiliated with Konami.

Data we process

Account holders

  • Account: email address; bcrypt password hash (email/password accounts) or linked OAuth identity (provider, provider user id, provider email when available); account creation and email-verification timestamps; optional trade share slug and public display name.
  • User content: collection items (including notes), folders, decks and deck cards, favorites, card tags, and saved search presets.
  • Authentication token: a JWT is stored in your browser localStorage so you stay signed in. It contains your user id and expiry only.

Public trade order requests (buyers)

  • If you submit an order request on a trade page, the contact details you choose to provide (name, email, phone, address) and line comments/offers are emailed to the seller via Brevo.
  • Those buyer contact fields are not stored in our application database. The seller’s account email is the message recipient (visible to Brevo as processor).
  • Submitting an order requires an explicit consent checkbox. Consent is a gate for that request only and is not stored as a separate audit log.

Security and abuse prevention

  • Rate-limit counters keyed by IP and/or email (for login, registration, verification, trade orders, and similar).
  • Pending email verification records (email, password hash, OTP hash, expiry) until you verify or the record expires and is cleaned up.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile may process a captcha token (and related technical data such as IP) on signup and trade order forms when enabled.

Processors and recipients

  • Brevo — transactional email (verification codes; trade order requests to sellers).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — bot protection when enabled.
  • OAuth providers (Google, Discord, GitHub, and/or Microsoft when enabled) — social sign-in.
  • Hosting and database — application host and PostgreSQL (e.g. Render / Neon as configured for the deployment).

Client storage (no marketing cookies)

We do not use marketing or analytics cookies. The browser may store:

  • JWT session token and currency preference in localStorage (main app).
  • Trade cart contents in sessionStorage (trade page).

These are essential for the service to function.

Legal bases (summary)

  • Account and user content: performance of the service you request (contract / pre-contractual steps) and related legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention).
  • Trade order contact fields: your consent for that request.
  • Rate limits and captcha: legitimate interests in protecting the service.

Retention

  • Account and user content: until you delete your account (or we remove it if required).
  • Pending verification: until successful verification or expiry/cleanup.
  • Rate-limit rows: sliding windows only; email-keyed rows are removed when you delete your account.
  • Email copies held by Brevo: according to Brevo’s processing terms / DPA with the operator.
  • Server logs (if any): short operational retention; we do not intentionally log buyer trade PII in application logs.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including GDPR where it applies), you may have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability.

  • Export (portability): signed-in users can download a JSON package of their account data (profile, OAuth links, collection, folders, decks, favorites, tags, search presets) via the app. Collection CSV export remains available as a format-specific collection download (not a full personal-data package).
  • Erasure: signed-in users can delete their account in the app (password confirmation, or email confirmation for social-sign-in-only accounts). Related user content is removed via database cascades; pending verification and email-keyed rate limits for that address are cleaned up.
  • Trade consent: do not submit an order request if you do not agree; there is nothing further to “withdraw” for that one-off email after it is sent.
